Significance

A versatile free flap based on the descending branch of the lateral circumflex femoral artery; with little donor-site morbidity, it is a mainstay of modern reconstruction.

Historical note: background to the “world first”

The original paper on the ALT flap, which spread worldwide as an “all-round flap.”
