Original paper and source

Harii K, Ohmori K, Ohmori SSuccessful clinical transfer of ten free flaps by microvascular anastomosesPlastic and Reconstructive SurgeryView original source ↗

Significance

Performed 10 consecutive successful free flap transfers, showing that the free flap is a reproducible clinical technique and decisively driving its adoption.

Historical note: background to the “world first”

An early large successful series that established the clinical reliability of free flaps (PRS 1974;53(3):259-270). Representative of the dawn of microsurgery in Japan.
